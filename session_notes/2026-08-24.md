## Work completed

- Clarified assumption A0 ("conditional-effect transportability given X") vs.
  the confounding-identification assumption (`ass:causal`) — distinct kinds of
  sufficiency (internal-validity ignorability vs. cross-population CATE
  invariance); grounded in the transportability literature (Bareinboim-Pearl,
  Dahabreh, Colnet et al.).
- Clarified `\IF_{\tau_a}(O;x)` (equation 24, Lemma A1 in `theory.tex`) is a
  *localized*, per-covariate-value AIPW score — not the marginal ATE
  influence function, and not the same object as the aggregate
  `psi_S[,m]`/`psi_Theta` used in the main estimator
  (`R/tv_ball_correlation_IF_adaptive.R`).
- Oracle consultation #1: assessed "resample individuals, not covariate
  values" as a way to drop A0. Result: the sigma(X)-projection of any richer
  reweighting is a TV contraction (attains no new estimand values); A0 is the
  identification frontier, not a convenience. Constructive path: a full-data
  ball with unmeasured modifier U gives
  `Cov(Delta_S,Delta_Y) = Cov_X(tau_S_bar,tau_Y_bar) [identified] + E_X[Cov(tau_S,tau_Y|X)] [unidentified]`,
  the second term boundable via Cauchy-Schwarz from within-arm conditional
  variances. Recommended as a partial-identification/sensitivity-analysis
  extension, not a new estimator.
- Decided against pursuing the sharp estimability elbow (`s_S+s_Y>d/2`) via
  HOIF/undersmoothing (no software backing, separate hard research problem).
  Instead added Remark `rem:kernel-IF-concrete` to `inst/paper/theory.tex`

  X via a kernel/local-polynomial estimator, and showing Lemma A1's assumed
  `o_P(n^{-1/4})` remainder is equivalent to the already-proven naive elbow
  (`s_S+s_Y>d`) under balanced smoothness — explicitly not closing the sharp
  elbow.
